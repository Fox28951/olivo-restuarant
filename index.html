About us

An Italian dinner with a view of the city

Olivo started with a simple idea: cook honest Italian food the way it is cooked at home in Tuscany, and serve it somewhere you want to stay a little longer.

Olive trees grow in the dining room, the wood-fired oven is always lit, and the floor-to-ceiling windows look out across the Thames to St Paul's. The bar stays open until the last guest leaves, and our sommelier will find a wine for any dish.

  • 40+wines by the glass
